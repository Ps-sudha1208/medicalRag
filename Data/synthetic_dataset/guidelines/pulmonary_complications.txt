POST-OPERATIVE PULMONARY COMPLICATION GUIDELINES

Common Complications:
- Atelectasis: Most common, POD 1-2
- Pneumonia: POD 3-5, fever + productive cough + infiltrate
- Pulmonary Embolism (PE): Sudden SpO2 drop, tachycardia, pleuritic pain
- Respiratory Failure: SpO2 < 90% on room air

SpO2 Action Thresholds:
- 95-100%: Normal, continue monitoring
- 92-94%: Supplemental O2, investigate cause
- 90-91%: HIGH alert — urgent respiratory review
- < 90%: CRITICAL — immediate intervention, ICU consideration

Respiratory Rate Alerts:
- > 20 breaths/min: Early warning sign
- > 25 breaths/min: HIGH — immediate assessment
- > 30 breaths/min: CRITICAL — potential respiratory failure

PE Risk Stratification (Post-Op):
- High Risk: Immobility > 3 days, prior DVT/PE, malignancy
- Prophylaxis: LMWH + compression stockings + early mobilization
- Diagnostic: D-dimer, CT pulmonary angiogram if suspected
