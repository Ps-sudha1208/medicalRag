SEPSIS CLINICAL GUIDELINES

Definition:
Sepsis is a life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host response to infection.

RED FLAG Criteria (CRITICAL Risk):
- Temperature > 38.5°C or < 36°C
- Heart Rate > 100 bpm
- Respiratory Rate > 20 breaths/min
- WBC > 12.0 x10^9/L or < 4.0 x10^9/L
- SpO2 < 92%
- Systolic BP < 90 mmHg

Management:
- Immediate blood cultures x2
- IV broad-spectrum antibiotics within 1 hour
- IV fluid resuscitation: 30ml/kg crystalloid
- ICU referral if organ dysfunction present
- Serial lactate monitoring

Post-Operative Sepsis Risk:
- POD 1-3: Highest risk window for anastomotic leak
- Wound infection signs: erythema, purulent discharge, dehiscence
- Monitor daily: temperature, WBC, CRP, lactate
