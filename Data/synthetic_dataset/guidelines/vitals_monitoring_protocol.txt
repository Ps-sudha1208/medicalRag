POST-OPERATIVE VITALS MONITORING PROTOCOL

Monitoring Frequency:
- POD 0-2: Every 2 hours
- POD 3-5: Every 4 hours
- POD 6+: Every 8 hours if stable

Normal Ranges (Post-Operative Adult):
- Temperature: 36.0 - 37.5°C
- Heart Rate: 60 - 100 bpm
- Blood Pressure Systolic: 90 - 160 mmHg
- Blood Pressure Diastolic: 60 - 100 mmHg
- Respiratory Rate: 12 - 20 breaths/min
- SpO2: >= 95%
- Pain Score: Target < 4

Lab Alert Thresholds:
- WBC > 12.0: Investigate for infection
- WBC > 15.0: HIGH suspicion of systemic infection
- Hemoglobin < 8.0: Transfusion threshold review
- Hemoglobin < 7.0: Urgent transfusion consideration

Escalation Protocol:
- Any single CRITICAL vital: Notify senior immediately
- Two or more HIGH vitals: Urgent medical review within 30 min
- Trending deterioration over 2 readings: Escalate regardless of absolute values

Pain Management:
- Score 0-3: Continue current regimen
- Score 4-6: Step up analgesia, reassess in 1 hour
- Score 7-10: Urgent pain team review, consider IV opioids
