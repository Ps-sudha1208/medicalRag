POST-OPERATIVE WOUND CARE GUIDELINES

Wound Assessment (Daily):
- Inspect for erythema, edema, warmth, discharge
- Dehiscence: separation of wound edges — escalate immediately
- Surgical site infection (SSI): redness + purulent drainage + fever

Risk Tiers:
LOW:
  - Clean wound, no signs of infection
  - Pain Score 0-3, afebrile, normal WBC

MEDIUM:
  - Mild erythema, serous discharge
  - Pain Score 4-6, low-grade fever (37.5-38.5°C)
  - WBC 10-12 x10^9/L

HIGH:
  - Purulent discharge, significant erythema
  - Pain Score 7-8, fever > 38.5°C
  - WBC 12-15 x10^9/L

CRITICAL:
  - Wound dehiscence or evisceration
  - Necrotizing fasciitis suspected
  - Pain Score > 8, systemic sepsis signs
  - WBC > 15 x10^9/L or < 4 x10^9/L
  - SpO2 < 92%, HR > 120 bpm

Interventions:
- Wound swab for culture and sensitivity
- Surgical review within 2 hours for HIGH/CRITICAL
- Negative pressure wound therapy if indicated
